📝 听力原文

Scientists at the National Aeronautics and Space Administration are testing a next-generation computer chip designed to give spacecraft the ability to operate far more independently in deep space. The radiation-hardened processor shows performance levels hundreds of times beyond current spaceflight computers. Current space computers are decades behind modern consumer technology because they must be built to withstand extreme radiation, temperature fluctuations, and cosmic rays. The new chip uses a special design that can detect and correct errors caused by radiation in real time, allowing it to run faster without crashing. According to NASA engineers, this breakthrough could enable future missions to Mars and beyond to make real-time decisions without waiting for instructions from Earth, which can take up to twenty minutes each way.
