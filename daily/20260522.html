In recent years, the concept of green buildings has gained widespread attention around the world. Green buildings not only focus on energy conservation and environmental protection but also emphasize creating healthy and comfortable living environments for occupants. By adopting solar panels, rainwater collection systems, and natural ventilation designs, green buildings can significantly reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions. The Chinese government has introduced a series of policies to encourage developers to adopt green building standards and regard them as an important component of sustainable urban development. A growing body of research indicates that people living in green buildings not only enjoy lower energy costs but also have higher life satisfaction and work efficiency.

Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming the landscape of education, bringing both exciting opportunities and significant challenges that deserve careful consideration. On the positive side, AI-powered tools can personalize learning experiences to an unprecedented degree. Intelligent tutoring systems can adapt to each student's pace and learning style, providing targeted practice where it is needed most. Automated grading systems free teachers from repetitive tasks, allowing them to focus on more meaningful interactions with students. Furthermore, AI can make quality educational resources accessible to students in remote areas who might otherwise lack access to experienced teachers. However, the integration of AI into education also raises serious concerns. Over-reliance on automated systems may diminish students' critical thinking abilities and reduce meaningful human interaction in the learning process. Privacy issues surrounding the collection of student data require robust safeguards. Additionally, the digital divide means that students from disadvantaged backgrounds may be left further behind if AI tools remain accessible only to well-funded schools. In my view, AI should serve as a supplement to, rather than a replacement for, human teachers. The goal should be to harness AI's efficiency while preserving the irreplaceable human elements of education—mentorship, inspiration, and emotional connection.
