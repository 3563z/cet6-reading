NASA's Next-Gen Space Computer Chip

第 1 题
What is the main advantage of NASA's new computer chip?
A. It is cheaper to produce than current chips
B. It can operate hundreds of times faster than current space computers
C. It uses less power than any existing processor
D. It can communicate with Earth instantly
✅ 正确答案：B
新芯片的性能比现有航天计算机提升数百倍。目前太空中的计算机因为要承受极端辐射、温度变化和宇宙射线，技术远落后于消费级产品。
第 2 题
Why are current space computers so far behind modern technology?
A. NASA lacks funding for new technology
B. Space agencies prefer older, reliable systems
C. They must withstand extreme radiation and temperature changes
D. Modern chips cannot fit inside spacecraft
✅ 正确答案：C
航天计算机必须承受极端辐射、温度波动和宇宙射线，因此设计上有很多限制，导致其技术水平落后消费级产品数十年。
第 3 题
How does the new chip handle radiation-related problems?
A. It detects and corrects errors in real time
B. It uses thicker shielding material
C. It avoids radiation by staying in low orbit
D. It shuts down temporarily when radiation is detected
✅ 正确答案：A
新芯片采用了特殊设计，可以实时检测并纠正辐射导致的错误(detect and correct errors caused by radiation in real time)，使其可以在不崩溃的情况下高速运行。
第 4 题
What benefit could this chip bring to future Mars missions?
A. Reducing launch costs significantly
B. Enabling faster communication with Earth
C. Eliminating the need for human astronauts
D. Allowing spacecraft to make real-time decisions independently
✅ 正确答案：D
未来火星任务可以自主做出实时决策，无需等待地球指令——每程通信延迟可能长达20分钟(up to twenty minutes each way)，自主决策能力至关重要。
第 5 题
The word "withstand" in the passage is closest in meaning to:
A. avoid
B. endure or resist
C. measure
D. generate
✅ 正确答案：B
"Withstand"意为"承受、抵抗"，指航天计算机必须能够耐受极端辐射和温度波动。文中"withstand extreme radiation, temperature fluctuations, and cosmic rays"正描述了芯片需要承受的恶劣太空环境。

📝 听力原文

Scientists at the National Aeronautics and Space Administration are testing a next-generation computer chip designed to give spacecraft the ability to operate far more independently in deep space. The radiation-hardened processor, known as the High-Performance Spaceflight Computing chip, is showing performance levels hundreds of times beyond current spaceflight computers. Current space computers are decades behind modern consumer technology because they must be built to withstand extreme radiation, temperature fluctuations, and cosmic rays. The new chip uses a special design that can detect and correct errors caused by radiation in real time, allowing it to run faster without crashing. According to NASA engineers, this breakthrough could enable future missions to Mars and beyond to make real-time decisions without waiting for instructions from Earth, which can take up to twenty minutes each way.

🔬 科技 · 衰老研究

Gut Microbiome Particles Linked to Aging and Inflammation

A new study published in the journal Nature Aging has revealed that microscopic particles from the gut may actively drive inflammation and chronic diseases associated with aging. The research, conducted by a team at the Mayo Clinic, discovered that tiny particles released by gut bacteria can enter the bloodstream and trigger an immune response that accelerates age-related decline. Remarkably, the study also found that gut particles from young animals appeared to counter some of these effects when transferred to older ones.

The particles in question are known as extracellular vesicles — tiny membrane-bound structures that carry proteins, lipids, and genetic material between cells. While scientists have known about these vesicles for years, their role in the aging process has remained unclear. The Mayo Clinic team, led by Dr. James Morrison, tracked these particles in mice and found that their composition changes dramatically with age. Young mice produced vesicles rich in anti-inflammatory molecules, while older mice produced vesicles that promoted inflammation and tissue damage.

When the researchers transferred vesicles from young mice into older ones, they observed a reduction in markers of chronic inflammation and improved function in several organs, including the liver and kidneys. "It's as if we transplanted a young gut's signaling system into an older body," said Dr. Morrison. "The aging gut isn't just a passive organ that wears out over time — it's actively sending signals that influence the health of the entire body." The findings suggest that interventions targeting gut-derived vesicles could open new avenues for treating age-related diseases.

The study has generated significant interest in the scientific community, with researchers around the world now investigating whether similar mechanisms operate in humans. If confirmed, the discovery could lead to new therapeutic approaches that target the gut microbiome not just for digestive health, but for slowing the aging process itself. "We've known for years that the gut microbiome is important for health," said Dr. Morrison. "But this study suggests it may play a far more active role in how we age than anyone previously anticipated."

🌍 社会 · 国际关系

Trump's China Visit Redefines Bilateral Relations

President Donald Trump returned to the United States on Friday after a two-day visit to Beijing, where he held extensive talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping. The summit, which included both formal meetings at the Great Hall of the People and a private visit to Zhongnanhai, has been widely described by analysts as a turning point in the complex relationship between the world's two largest economies. Both sides issued a joint statement emphasizing a "new positioning" of bilateral ties.

The discussions covered a wide range of issues, from trade imbalances and tariff policies to technology cooperation and global security. While details of the private meeting between the two leaders remain undisclosed, public statements from both sides suggested a mutual desire to de-escalate trade tensions that have characterized their relationship in recent years. Xi reportedly raised his earlier observation about the "Thucydides Trap" — the idea that a rising power and an established power are often drawn into conflict — framing it as a question that both nations must answer together.

Economic analysts have noted that the timing of the summit is particularly significant, coming as both countries face domestic economic pressures. The United States is grappling with persistent inflation and a manufacturing slowdown, while China's post-pandemic recovery has been uneven. "Neither side can afford the luxury of a prolonged trade war," said Dr. Li Wei, a professor of international relations at Peking University. "This summit signals a pragmatic shift in approach from both sides."

The visit also included cultural elements, with Trump attending a state banquet featuring traditional Chinese cuisine and a brief tour of the Forbidden City. American media noted the striking contrast between this diplomatic warmth and the confrontational rhetoric that dominated the two countries' relationship during the previous administration. Whether this rapprochement will translate into lasting policy changes remains to be seen, but the images of two leaders walking together through Beijing's historic sites have already reshaped the global narrative around US-China relations.
