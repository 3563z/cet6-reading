NASA Space Computer Chip

Question 1
A. It is cheaper to produce
B. It is smaller in size
C. It delivers much greater computing power
D. It uses less electricity
✅ 正确答案：C
新芯片的计算能力是现有系统的数百倍(hundreds of times greater)，因为它采用了特殊的纠错机制，可以在不崩溃的情况下高速运行。
Question 2
A. They are designed for different purposes
B. They must survive extreme space conditions
C. NASA cannot afford to upgrade them
D. They were built decades ago
✅ 正确答案：B
现有航天计算机必须承受极端太空环境(extreme conditions of space)，因此设计上有很多限制，技术落后消费级产品数十年。
Question 3
A. A special error-correction mechanism
B. Thicker protective shielding
C. Lower operating temperatures
D. Simpler programming software
✅ 正确答案：A
秘密在于特殊的纠错机制(special error-correction mechanism)，它可以实时检测并修复宇宙辐射导致的问题，使处理器能够高速运行而不崩溃。
Question 4
A. Limited fuel supply
B. High equipment costs
C. Extreme temperature changes
D. Communication delays with Earth
✅ 正确答案：D
深空任务面临的主要挑战是通信延迟（communication delay），以火星任务为例，信号单程需要20分钟，来回40分钟。
Question 5
A. Human astronauts will no longer be needed
B. Travel time to Mars will be cut in half
C. Spacecraft can make decisions on their own
D. Communication with Earth will become instant
✅ 正确答案：C
新芯片将使航天器能够独立分析情况并做出决策(analyze situations and make decisions independently)，极大地提升应对突发挑战的能力。

📝 听力原文

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration is testing a revolutionary new computer processor that could transform the way spacecraft operate in deep space. Unlike traditional space computers, which are decades behind modern consumer technology in terms of performance, this new radiation-hardened chip is designed to withstand the extreme conditions of space while delivering computing power hundreds of times greater than current systems. The secret lies in a special error-correction mechanism that can detect and fix problems caused by cosmic radiation in real time, allowing the processor to run at high speeds without crashing. Currently, space missions rely heavily on constant communication with Earth for decision making. However, as spacecraft travel farther into the solar system, the delay in communication becomes a critical problem. For a mission to Mars, for example, a signal can take up to twenty minutes to reach Earth, and another twenty minutes for a response to return. This means that if a spacecraft encounters an unexpected obstacle or needs to make a quick decision, it must either wait for instructions or rely on pre-programmed responses. The new chip would change this by enabling spacecraft to analyze situations and make decisions independently, vastly improving their ability to respond to unexpected challenges in real time.

📝 听力原文

The Eurovision Song Contest reached its 70th anniversary this year, with performers from 26 countries competing in Vienna, Austria. What began in 1956 as a modest television experiment involving just seven countries has grown into one of the world's largest live music events, attracting over 180 million viewers globally. This year's competition was notable not only for the quality of performances but also for the way several artists used their platform to express views on social and political issues. Security was tight due to recent tensions across Europe, yet thousands of fans gathered outside the stadium despite rainy weather. Australia was represented by Delta Goodrem, who performed a powerful ballad about unity and peace, and was considered one of the favorites. The event has evolved significantly since its early days, when it was primarily a Western European affair. Today, it includes participants from countries as far as Australia and Israel, reflecting the contest's growing global reach and cultural significance.

Gut Microbiome Particles Linked to Aging and Inflammation

A new study published in the journal Nature Aging has revealed that microscopic particles from the gut may actively drive inflammation and chronic diseases associated with aging. The research, conducted by a team at the Mayo Clinic, discovered that tiny particles released by gut bacteria can enter the bloodstream and trigger an immune response that accelerates age-related decline. Remarkably, the study also found that gut particles from young animals appeared to counter some of these effects when transferred to older ones.

The particles in question are known as extracellular vesicles — tiny membrane-bound structures that carry proteins, lipids, and genetic material between cells. While scientists have known about these vesicles for years, their role in the aging process has remained unclear. The Mayo Clinic team, led by Dr. James Morrison, tracked these particles in mice and found that their composition changes dramatically with age. Young mice produced vesicles rich in anti-inflammatory molecules, while older mice produced vesicles that promoted inflammation and tissue damage.

When the researchers transferred vesicles from young mice into older ones, they observed a reduction in markers of chronic inflammation and improved function in several organs, including the liver and kidneys. "It's as if we transplanted a young gut's signaling system into an older body," said Dr. Morrison. The findings suggest that interventions targeting gut-derived vesicles could open new avenues for treating age-related diseases.

The study has generated significant interest in the scientific community, with researchers around the world now investigating whether similar mechanisms operate in humans. If confirmed, the discovery could lead to new therapeutic approaches that target the gut microbiome not just for digestive health, but for slowing the aging process itself.

🌍 社会 · 国际关系

Trump's China Visit Redefines Bilateral Relations

President Donald Trump returned to the United States on Friday after a two-day visit to Beijing, where he held extensive talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping. The summit, which included both formal meetings at the Great Hall of the People and a private visit to Zhongnanhai, has been widely described by analysts as a turning point in the complex relationship between the world's two largest economies.

The discussions covered a wide range of issues, from trade imbalances and tariff policies to technology cooperation and global security. Xi reportedly raised his earlier observation about the "Thucydides Trap" — the idea that a rising power and an established power are often drawn into conflict — framing it as a question that both nations must answer together.

Economic analysts have noted that the timing of the summit is particularly significant, coming as both countries face domestic economic pressures. The United States is grappling with persistent inflation, while China's post-pandemic recovery has been uneven. "Neither side can afford the luxury of a prolonged trade war," said Dr. Li Wei of Peking University.

The visit also included cultural elements, with Trump attending a state banquet featuring traditional Chinese cuisine and a brief tour of the Forbidden City. Whether this rapprochement will translate into lasting policy changes remains to be seen, but the images of two leaders walking together through Beijing's historic sites have already reshaped the global narrative around US-China relations.
